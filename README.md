A hand-written two-sentence horror story for make.fun's "Two-Sentence Horror" bounty: a father teaches his daughter to check under her bed every night so nothing can hide there — until one night she smiles and says the thing under the bed has been waiting for him to check too. `main.py` writes the story, exactly two sentences, deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
